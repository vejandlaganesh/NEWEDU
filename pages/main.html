Good to know

Scholarships, internships & certifications

A starting point for funding, real-world experience and credentials — worth exploring alongside any stream.

Scholarships

  • National Scholarship Portal (Govt. of India) — merit & means-based scholarships across states
  • State-level merit scholarships through your school/college
  • Institution-specific scholarships offered by many colleges and universities

Internships

  • Internshala — India's largest student internship marketplace
  • AICTE Internship Portal for technical students
  • Direct outreach to startups and local businesses in your field of interest

Certifications worth exploring

  • NPTEL / SWAYAM — free courses from top Indian institutes
  • Coursera & Google/Meta career certificates for tech & digital skills
  • Skill India (NSDC) certifications for hands-on trades
